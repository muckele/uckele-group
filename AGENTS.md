## Product roadmap and current-task boundaries

For product planning or feature work, read:
`docs/roadmap/2026-09-20-uckele-group-product-vision-and-roadmap.md`.

This is a dated vision and research reference, not permission to execute
every listed phase. Follow the current task's approved scope, specification,
plan, addenda, and authorization. Report material conflicts rather than
silently overriding them.

Before proposing new infrastructure, inspect and reuse the existing CRM,
Acquisition Inbox, Command Center, CIM lifecycle, communication history,
outbox, compose/reply, inbound handling, suppression, and secure-document
mechanisms where suitable.

Distinguish committed code, locally reported work, deployed behavior, and
proposed features. Do not infer completion from a roadmap entry.

Roadmap adoption does not authorize deployment, production repair, mailbox
connection, broker sending, recipient-policy changes, document disclosure, or
automation activation.

Do not interrupt an active implementation task to pursue later roadmap
features unless the owner explicitly changes the task.
